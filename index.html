      A live picture of severe weather across Texas, built from public government data and reports from neighbors. It started in Dallas–Fort Worth; some features (DART rail, a few named gauges) are DFW-only for now.

      • In danger now? Call 911. This map does not contact emergency services.
      • Warnings come from the National Weather Service. Keep Wireless Emergency Alerts on; this map can lag behind official warnings.
      • Gray means no data, not "safe." A road with no reports can still be flooded or iced.
      • Every reading shows its time. Old readings fade so you can tell what's current.

      Terms

      This is not an official warning service. Information can be wrong, incomplete or late. Use your own judgment and follow the National Weather Service, emergency managers and local officials.
