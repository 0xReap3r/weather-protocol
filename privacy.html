Privacy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Draft. This plain-language policy describes how the app works today. It hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer yet and may change before public launch.

Stormline is a free severe-weather map for Texas. It has no accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies and no analytics. Most of what you do stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your location. When you tap Near me, your browser shares your location with the app on your device. It isn't stored on our servers. (See "Spots you look up" below for the lookups it triggers.)
  • My places, settings and view choices (layers, view mode, language, folded legends) are saved in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Spots you look up

To show conditions, the app sends the coordinates of the spot you're viewing (the map center, a dropped pin or your Near me location) to public data services: the National Weather Service, NOAA, FEMA, Open-Meteo and, if you check a route, the OSRM routing service. They receive your device's IP address like any website you visit. They don't receive your name or any ID from us. Their own privacy policies apply.

When you search, city, county and ZIP code lookups run on your device or go to the U.S. Census Bureau's public map service. A street address goes through our server to the Census Bureau's geocoder to find it. We don't store searched addresses beyond a short-lived cache; recent searches are kept only on your device.

Who runs it

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the small server that checks ERCOT, sends push alerts and stores alert subscriptions. Like most hosts, it may log technical details such as IP addresses for security.
  • Supabase stores neighbor reports.
  • Push notifications travel through your browser maker's push service (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft).

We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Your choices

  • Use the map without Near me, reports or alerts. Everything else still works.
  • Turn alerts off in My places to delete your subscription.
  • Clear your browser's site data to remove saved places and settings.

Children

Stormline isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Questions or requests: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
